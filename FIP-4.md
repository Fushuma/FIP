# Fushuma Improvement Proposal #4

The FIP-2 established Fushuma as **a scalable and cost-effective platform** by delivering:

- Fushuma Mainnet. ([Grant #8](https://github.com/Fushuma/Dev_grants/issues/8))
- A Multi-chain Launchpad. ([Grant #22](https://github.com/Fushuma/Dev_grants/issues/22) & [Grant #32](https://github.com/Fushuma/Dev_grants/issues/32))
- A Cross-chain Bridge. ([Grant #28](https://github.com/Fushuma/Dev_grants/issues/28))
- The First dApp. ([Grant #26](https://github.com/Fushuma/Dev_grants/issues/26))
- Marketing Reach. ([Grant #19](https://github.com/Fushuma/Dev_grants/issues/19) & [Campaign #1](https://github.com/Fushuma/FIP/issues/3#issuecomment-2539446103), [Campaign #2](https://github.com/Fushuma/FIP/issues/3#issuecomment-2950064557))

An overview of the milestones and financial details from our successful FIP-2 funding round is available [here](https://fushuma.com/pow-security-meets-l2-speed/).

## Why This Round?
To sustain momentum and fund the next wave of features, FIP 4 seeks to raise **$112,500** by selling **75 million FUMA at $0.0015 each**.

There is no minimum investment, and every community member can participate directly through the [Fushuma Launchpad](https://launchpad.fushuma.com/).

Proceeds will allow Fushuma to:

- Complete staking contract development and deliver a user interface.
- Execute scheduled airdrops to early adopters.
- Seed 3–5 high-impact community projects via grants.
- Amplify awareness through targeted marketing campaigns.
- Maintain and scale core infrastructure.
- Onboard strategic partners.

## What Is the Plan?
We propose the following allocation of funds:
**I. Staking Protocol & User Interface**
Decentralize the token distribution by allowing the community to stake FUMA. We will develop, audit, and deploy the Fushuma staking smart contracts and build a dedicated UI.

**II. Airdrop Execution**
As per the Fushuma tokenomics, we’ll design and distribute token airdrops. Eligibility and the distribution process will be revealed at the start of the Airdrop phase.

**III. Community Project Grants**
We’ll allocate grants to 3–5 community-proposed projects. Each proposal will undergo community review and milestone-based funds release.

**IV. Marketing Campaigns**
This budget covers:

- Banner Ads on top crypto websites.
- Short-Form Videos (Instagram/YouTube Shorts).
- Sponsored Articles on leading publications with backlinks and homepage pins.

**V. Infrastructure Maintenance & Scaling**
Our partner, Zeeve, will be responsible for maintaining and updating the Fushuma infrastructure to ensure seamless operation and top-notch security of the Fushuma blockchain. A portion of the funds will be used to cover M365, domains, web hosting, and maintenance costs.

**VI. Partner Onboarding & Integration Support**
We’ll provide technical support and incentives to onboard wallets, bridges, and dApp teams to accelerate developer adoption.

## How to Participate in the FUMA Token Sale

- Visit [launchpad.fushuma.com](https://launchpad.fushuma.com/) starting on the official launch date, to be announced soon.

- Connect a compatible wallet (Metamask, TrustWallet, etc.).
- Select the FUSHUMA Sale pool.
- Enter any contribution amount in USDT (Ethereum, BNB Chain supported), and USDC (Base chain supported).
- Approve and sign the transaction. 
- That’s it, your FUMA tokens are delivered instantly.
